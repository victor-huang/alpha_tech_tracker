# Top-10 Performing US Stocks by Year (2020–2026)

As of 2026-11-08. Data: CSV in `top10_by_year_2020_2026.csv` (same folder).

## Method

Every active US-listed stock (about 12,600 tickers) was ranked by calendar-year return, then split into three size groups by market value at the start of the year.

- **Prices:** Alpaca daily bars, adjusted for splits and dividends (SIP feed).
- **Return:** prior year's last close to this year's last close. 2026 is year-to-date through Nov 6.
- **Size group:** Large ≥ $10B, Mid $2B–$10B, Small $300M–$2B. Market value = unadjusted close × shares outstanding (yfinance share history) on the prior year's last trading day.
- **Filters:** start price ≥ $2; median daily trading value ≥ $2M; traded ≥ 90% of the period's days; ETFs, leveraged products, warrants, units, SPAC shells and stocks under $300M excluded.

## Patterns

Each year's winners share one theme across all three size groups, and the theme changes almost every year.

| Year | Theme |
| --- | --- |
| 2020 | COVID tech, EVs, solar |
| 2021 | Meme stocks, reopening, commodities |
| 2022 | Oil, gas and tanker shipping |
| 2023 | AI, bitcoin miners, homebuilders |
| 2024 | AI, bitcoin, Argentina |
| 2025 | Memory and storage chips, gold miners, nuclear |
| 2026 YTD | Memory again, refiners, biotech |

- **Repeat winners:** NVDA (2021, 2023, 2024), MRNA (2020, 2021, 2026), PLTR, APP, SMCI, HOOD, SPOT, CRWD, CLS and RKLB. APP, CLS and RKLB moved up a size group between wins.
- **Small caps are mostly biotech news.** Six of the ten 2026 small caps and most of 2025's are drug developers moved by single trial results.

## Caveats and corrections

Four 2021 entries were corrected by hand; the rest come straight from the data.

| Ticker | Problem | Fix |
| --- | --- | --- |
| EXE (Chesapeake) | Bankruptcy: old shares cancelled, but the price series runs through, giving a false +456% | Dropped; CHRD moved into Small |
| GOLD | Ticker reused: A-Mark prices with Barrick's share count, so a ~$0.2B company showed as $45B | Dropped from Large; ON and BX moved in |
| LB | Ticker reused: the 2021 gain is L Brands (now BBWI), not LandBridge | Relabeled |
| LCID | Was the CCIV SPAC on Jan 1, 2021, worth about $2.1B (estimate) | Moved from Large to Mid |

- **Survivorship bias:** only tickers listed today are included. Winners later acquired or delisted are missing.
- **Renamed companies:** NXH, ACH, MPU, CNR and ASTH were matched to the earlier company by price. AGNT and FLNA could not be identified.
- **Rate limiting:** Yahoo throttled share lookups late in the run. A stock whose lookup failed would be skipped silently; spot-checks found no gaps.
- **Unverified recent data:** 2025–26 results come straight from the data, with no outside check.

## How to rerun

`alpha_tech_tracker/research/top_performers.py` repeats this ranking for any month, quarter or year. With no dates it ranks the most recently completed period. Run from the repo root with the virtualenv active and `ALPACA_API_KEY` / `ALPACA_SECRET_KEY` set.

| Cadence | Command |
| --- | --- |
| Monthly | `PYTHONPATH=. python -m alpha_tech_tracker.research.top_performers --period month` |
| Quarterly | `PYTHONPATH=. python -m alpha_tech_tracker.research.top_performers --period quarter` |
| Yearly | `PYTHONPATH=. python -m alpha_tech_tracker.research.top_performers --period year` |
| History + YTD | `... --period year --start 2020-01-01 --include-current` |

- **Output:** CSV + Markdown per run in `alpha_tech_tracker/research/top_performers_output/`.
- **Flags:** suspect rows (ticker reuse, bankruptcy splices, share-count jumps) are marked `[!flag]`, not dropped — check them by hand.
- **Caching:** daily prices are cached one file per month in `market_data/top_performers/daily_bars/` (2020-01 onward is already there). Finished months are never downloaded again; the current month is refreshed once a day. Older months are lined up with newer ones after later splits and dividends. Share counts are cached in `market_data/top_performers/shares.json` for 20 days. Change the location with `--cache-dir`.
- **Options:** `--top`, `--min-price` (default $2), `--min-dollar-volume` (default $2M), `--min-coverage` (default 90%).
