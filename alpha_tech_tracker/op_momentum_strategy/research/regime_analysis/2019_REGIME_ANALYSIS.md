## Key Patterns and Findings

### January 2019 — Fed Pivot Bull (Rising Curve, Peaks +3h)
- EOD WR: 64.4%, total P&L: **+37.45%** — one of the strongest January openings in the dataset
- Hold curve peaks at **+3h (+50.91%)** — unusual for bull months that usually peak at EOD or +5h
- Context: Jan 4 Jerome Powell speech signaled Fed pivot. Market had crashed ~20% in Dec 2018 (worst Dec since 1931). The OR screener captured the aggressive snap-back rally across growth/tech.
- 45 signals (2.1/day) — normal count, not inflated; quality was high as nearly every breakout worked
- Pre-session top-2 picked META (50% EOD) and AMD (45%) while signal analysis hit 64.4% — confirms the pre-session lag in sudden bull environments

### July 2019 — Extreme Late-Day Bull (Worst AM Win Rate in Dataset)
- +15m WR: **35.7%** — the single lowest +15m WR in any non-February month across all 8 years (2019–2026)
- +30m WR: **32.1%** — equally terrible, worse than the +15m
- EOD WR: 53.6%, total P&L: **+4.02%** — the signal flipped from strongly negative AM to mildly positive EOD
- Hold curve: -4.64% (+15m) → -0.07% (+1h) → +4.02% (EOD) — a 8.66 percentage point recovery over the afternoon
- Context: July 2019 was choppier than expected. Three Fed rate cuts were priced in; the July 31 cut was the first. The OR signals were consistently faded in the morning but price recovered by EOD.
- **Rule extension:** The "late-day bull" pattern can appear with +15m WRs as low as 35%. When you see terrible AM performance but EOD recovering above 50%, the signal is identifying real momentum that just needs the full day to resolve.

### September 2019 — September Fade Exception
- EOD WR: **48.1%** (below 50%) BUT EOD total P&L: **+8.34%** — positive despite sub-50% WR
- The win/loss asymmetry explains it: avg win at EOD +1.55% vs avg loss -0.85% → EV = +0.31% per signal despite losing more often
- Hold curve is rising into EOD: +5h +5.33%, +7h/EOD +8.34% — the momentum builds into close, not fading
- This is the **opposite** of the September fade pattern: here, the morning was flat (-0.31% at +15m) and the afternoon strengthened
- Context: Sep 3 the Fed cut again (Sep 18). Trade war had a lull (Oct deal hopes). Tech/growth resumed after Aug trade war volatility.
- **Rule update:** September "exit by +30m" default does NOT apply when the hold curve shows rising late-day EV. Confirm by checking avg win > avg loss even when WR < 50%.

### October 2019 — U-Curve Bull (Peaks at +2h)
- +15m WR: **44.4% (-2.27%)** — weak morning, same as the November and July pattern
- +2h peak: 58.3% / **+15.72%** — strongest window in the month
- EOD: 55.6% / +13.73% — solid close, but +2h was the optimal hold
- U-curve shape: terrible AM → improving midday → partial EOD fade from peak
- 36 signals (1.6/day) — rising count as trade deal optimism brought momentum back
- **October bull pattern confirmed** for 2019: 6th consecutive year (2014–2019) with positive October

### November 2019 — Low-WR Positive EV (Inverse of High-WR Trap)
- EOD WR: **38.9%** — fourth lowest in the dataset (only slightly above Dec 2022's 30.6%)
- EOD total P&L: **+8.72%** — positive despite winning less than 40% of the time
- How: avg win at EOD **+2.02%** vs avg loss **-0.89%** — wins are 2.3x larger than losses
- The +3h window was the most concentrated: +11.90% — this is where most of the EV concentrated
- This is the **inverse of the High-WR Trap** (where WR is high but EV is negative). Here WR is low but EV is positive because wins are outsized.
- Context: US-China Phase 1 deal progress; the few breakouts that worked ran very hard (trade deal sensitive names like QCOM, AMD, AVGO).
- **Rule addition:** Check avg win/loss ratio when EOD WR is between 35–45%. If avg win ≥ 2× avg loss, the signals are worth taking — the low WR is masking positive EV.

### December 2019 — Second Positive December in Dataset
- EOD WR: 62.5%, total P&L: **+9.06%** — clearly positive
- Macro catalyst: Dec 13 Phase 1 trade deal announcement triggered a strong Santa rally
- 40 signals (1.9/day) — rising count into year-end
- Hold curve is mostly flat-positive; +5h was the peak (+11.33%)
- **Second positive December in the dataset (2019 and 2020 are the exceptions; 5/7 years still negative)**
- Both exceptions were macro-catalyst driven: Dec 2019 (Phase 1 deal), Dec 2020 (vaccine approval)

- **Strongest month:** January (+37.45%) — Fed pivot-driven, rising curve to +3h
- **Most extreme AM-collapse:** July (+15m 35.7%, lowest non-Feb +15m WR in dataset) with full late-day recovery

- **Bear months were mild:** Only Mar (-2.01%) and Apr (-2.15%) were negative, and both were shallow. No month below -3%.
- **2019 was the mildest-bear year in the full dataset** — worst month was only -2.15%, consistent with a strong bull recovery year after Dec 2018
