## Key Patterns and Records

### February 2020 — Most Extreme Acute AM Bear in Dataset
- +15m WR: **23.5%** — the single lowest +15m win rate across all 7 years (2020–2026)
- Context: COVID fear began spreading in mid-February; any opening range breakout immediately reversed
- Signal count: only 17 (0.9/day) — the low count flagged the disrupted environment
- The rule "very low +15m + very low count → ACUTE bear" is validated at maximum intensity here

### March 2020 — Highest Single-Month Total P&L in Dataset
- EOD total P&L: **+50.0%** — the single largest total P&L across all 7 years
- Avg win per signal: +4.68% at EOD — four times the typical avg win in normal months
- Hold curve peaks at +5h (+62.9%) — holding 5 hours was optimal
- Context: the COVID crash created enormous intraday swings; the screener's OR breakout was capturing the violent daily bounces on the way down AND the rally days. Both directions produced massive moves.
- **Key insight:** High volatility amplifies the OR signal in BOTH directions. In March 2020, the breakout direction (long) happened to be net positive for the month despite a -34% market drawdown. This suggests the ticker list (growth/tech) was already bottoming while the broader market was still falling.

### September 2020 — Sharpest Single-Window Collapse in Dataset
- +15m: 66.0% (+4.3%), then +1h: **34.0% (-31.0%)** — a **32 percentage point drop** in one hold interval
- This is the most extreme AM-pop-fade recorded across all 84 months of data
- Context: September 2020 tech selloff after the summer AI/tech bubble of 2020; morning bounces reversed violently within 45 minutes
- Total P&L at +1h: -31.0% vs +15m: +4.3% — holding even 45 extra minutes cost 35 percentage points
- **Rule confirmed at maximum intensity:** In the AM-pop-fade pattern, exit at +15m is mandatory. The +1h window here was -31% on the long side → +31% on the short side.

### October 2020 — Highest +15m Win Rate in Dataset
- +15m WR: **75.0%** — the single highest +15m win rate across all 7 years
- The hold curve is strongly positive at all windows: 75% → 65.6% (+1h) → 68.8% (EOD)
- Total P&L at +5h: +29.4% — best short-to-mid hold in any October
- Context: October pre-election rally; every OR breakout in tech worked perfectly

### December 2020 — Only Positive December in the Full Dataset
- EOD total P&L: **+20.3%** — the only December with positive P&L across 2020–2025
- Context: COVID vaccine deployment, stimulus, and growth/tech continuation made Dec 2020 a broad bull month
- **The December bear rule applies in 5 of 6 years (2020 is the exception)**. When December breaks positive, it tends to be driven by a macro catalyst (vaccine in 2020).
