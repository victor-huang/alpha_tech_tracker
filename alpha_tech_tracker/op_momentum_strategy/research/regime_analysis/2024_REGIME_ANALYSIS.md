## Regime Patterns — New and Confirmed

### ✅ Pattern Confirmed: Strong Bull with Rising Hold Curve (Jan–Feb 2024)
January is the most extreme example of the late-day compounding bull across all three years:
- +15m: only 46.7% (weakest short-term reading of a bull month in the entire dataset)
- EOD: 71.1% (strongest EOD reading of any month across 2024–2026, tied with Apr 2025)
- Slope: nearly monotonically rising from +15m through +5h — every additional hour of holding adds win rate
- The hold curve shows pure momentum accumulation: the OR breakout needs time to be confirmed, then compounds
- Total P&L at +15m: only -1.6%, but at EOD: +23.4% — **holding all day nearly doubles the signal value**

February shows the same pattern less extremely: EOD 66.7% vs +15m 54.5%.

**Rule:** When EOD WR is ≥15 points higher than +15m WR and the curve is monotonically rising, maximize hold duration. This is the most profitable configuration in the dataset.

### ✅ Pattern Confirmed: Deepest AM-Pop Fade (Aug 2024)
The sharpest single-month reversal in the entire 3-year dataset:
- +15m: 64.3% (very strong — looks like a bull month)
- +30m: 50.0% (drops 14 points in one interval)
- +5h: 38.1% (continues falling — lowest intraday window of any month across all years)
- EOD: 40.5%, total P&L: **-28.8%**
- The opening momentum is genuine but completely unsustainable
- **Short strategy:** short at OR-close, hold to +5h → flip of -31.4% long total to +31.4% on short

Pre-session top-2 was a notable exception: 54.5% EOD (+12.9% total), diverging +41.7% from signal analysis. The 20d historical best picks maintained their edge even as daily breakout signals failed completely.

### ✅ Pattern Confirmed: Post-Event Bull (Nov 2024 — Election Rally)
- Nov 2024: 58.0% EOD, +42.9% — second-highest EOD total P&L in 2024
- Hold curve peaks at +1h (70.0% — highest single window across all 36 months)
- Nov 2025 was a High-WR/Negative-EV trap but Nov 2024 was the real deal with positive expected value at every window

### ✅ Pattern Confirmed: December Bear
- Dec 2024: 47.9% EOD, -16.3%
- Dec 2025: 36.0% EOD, -25.7%
- Two consecutive Decembers show bear conditions — year-end positioning / tax-loss selling appears to be a reliable headwind for OR momentum signals
- **Rule:** Reduce size in December and expect short-side edge at EOD

### ✅ Pattern Confirmed: Signal Count as Quality Indicator
March 2024 had only **17 signals / 0.9 per day** — the lowest single-month count across all three years:
- +15m: 76.5%, +30m: 76.5% — also the highest short-term win rates across the entire dataset
- Result: when the market produces very few setups, those setups are exceptionally high quality
- However: EOD faded to 52.9% — the scarcity means no sustained trend, just sharp punctual moves
- **Rule:** Ultra-low signal count (< 1/day) + very high +15m WR (>70%) = scalp only, do not hold

## New Observations from 2024

### 🆕 April Was a Bear (No Universal April Recovery)
The 2025 and 2026 data suggested "April recovery" as a seasonal rule. **2024 breaks it:**
- Apr 2024: 47.9% EOD, -14.1% total — a clear bear month
- Hold curve declines from +15m (58.3%) to +3h (33.3%) — worst midday window of the year
- The April recovery in 2025 was driven by Liberation Day (Apr 9 tariff pause), not seasonality
- The April recovery in 2026 was a technical rebound from the Feb-Mar crash, not seasonality

**Revised rule:** April recovery is **macro-event dependent**, not seasonal. Do not assume April is bullish without confirmation from the first 2–3 trading days.

### 🆕 Pre-Session Top-2 Can Decouple from Signal Analysis
In August and September 2024, a striking divergence appeared:

| Month | Signal EOD WR | Signal EOD Total | Pre-Session EOD WR | Pre-Session EOD Total | Gap     |
|-------|--------------|-----------------|--------------------|-----------------------|---------|
| Aug   | 40.5%        | -28.8%          | 54.5%              | +12.9%                | +41.7%  |
| Sep   | 44.4%        | +6.3%           | 60.0%              | +8.7%                 | Aligned |
| Jun   | 50.0%        | +0.8%           | 65.8%              | +18.8%                | +18.0%  |

The pre-session top-2 (9:25 entry based on 20d EOD win rate) outperformed signal analysis by over 40 percentage points in August. The 20d lookback identifies tickers with durable multi-week momentum — which survives AM-pop-fade conditions better than the daily OR breakout signal.

**Practical implication:** Consider running pre-session top-2 as a **separate, independent signal** that does not follow the same long/short regime decisions as the OR breakout signal analysis. The pre-session picks may still be worth taking LONG even when signal analysis says BEAR (Aug 2024 being the clearest example).

### 🆕 November 2024 — Hold Window Shape Anomaly
November has the highest single win rate across any window in the entire 3-year dataset: **+1h at 70.0%**. But by EOD it falls to 58.0%. This is the opposite of January's rising curve.

- Nov 2024 shape: rises sharply to +1h (70%), then declines to EOD (58%)
- Best hold: +3h–+4h (68.0%)
- Do NOT hold all day — diminishing returns after +1h in November

This contrasts with Oct 2024 which is flat (57–63%) and Nov 2025 which is a high-WR/negative-EV trap. November is volatile — check expected value, not just win rate.
