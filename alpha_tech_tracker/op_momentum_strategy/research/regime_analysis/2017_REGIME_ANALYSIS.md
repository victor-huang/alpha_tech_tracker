## Key Patterns and Findings

### January 2017 — Rising-Curve Bull (8th Consecutive Positive January)
- +15m WR: 64.5% (+6.61%) — strong morning entry
- EOD WR: 67.7%, EOD P&L: +15.09% — best window is EOD
- Hold curve rises consistently: +15m→+1h dip, then climbs to +4h (+11.04%), +5h (+11.86%), EOD (+15.09%)
- **January bull confirmed: 8/9 years now** (only exception would need to check 2016)
- The slight dip at +1h is a head-fake; holding through to EOD was optimal

### March 2017 — AM Pop-Fade (5th Consecutive March Caution)
- +15m: 48.5% (+1.63%) — modest AM, barely positive
- +1h: 36.4% (-0.13%) — collapsed 12pp from +15m
- EOD: 39.4% (-3.51%) — negative
- Context: Trump healthcare failure (AHCA pulled Mar 24). Political uncertainty killed afternoon follow-through.
- This makes 5 consecutive March caution years (2017–2021 all show AM fade); confirms March rule strongly
- **March rule**: exit by +30m maximum; the PM session consistently reverses morning gains

### April 2017 — Persistent Bear (Rare +15m-Negative Month)
- +15m WR: **42.3% (-1.95%)** — even the 15-minute hold was losing; one of only 2 months where +15m P&L is negative in a "caution" month
- EOD: 34.6% (-9.99%) — sharp persistent bear
- Context: Geopolitical risk (Syria strikes Apr 6-7, NK tension) and Trump tax plan disappointment. Tech sold off through April.
- **April bear pattern extends:** 2017 joins 2018, 2019, 2022, 2024 — 5 of 9 Aprils are losing months
- This is the first time in the dataset where even +15m fails — meaning signal count is high but every exit was losing; classic panic-selling regime

### May 2017 — Uniformly Negative (WR Deceives)
- EOD WR: 52.9% (above 50%) but EOD P&L: -4.64% — the WR looks OK but avg loss > avg win at all windows
- +15m P&L: -6.41% (worst single +15m result in any positive-WR month)
- All windows from +15m to EOD are negative (-4.64% to -8.28%)
- Context: Comey firing (May 9), Trump Russia investigation fears. High-beta tech underperformed despite S&P staying near flat.
- **Key lesson**: EOD WR above 50% does NOT guarantee positive P&L if avg win/loss ratio is unfavorable. Check P&L not just WR.

### June-July 2017 — Patience Rising-Curve Bulls
- **June**: +15m = -2.32%, EOD = +8.70% — must hold past +1h to profit; curve rises from +1h onward
- **July**: +15m = -2.92%, EOD = +21.13% — strongest month of the year; curve rises all day; +6h peak (+21.65%)
- Both months: the **+15m signal is a false negative** — the OR fires correctly but the first 15 minutes is a shakeout; real momentum builds through the morning and afternoon
- This is the clearest example of why "rising-curve bull = hold to EOD" is the right rule
- Context: H2 2017 was the peak of the low-vol FAANG rally. AMD/AVGO/TSLA all had major runs in this window.

### August 2017 — Low-Signal U-Curve (North Korea Tension)
- Signal count: **0.7/day** (17 signals in 23 trading days) — lowest signal count in any month of the 2017 dataset
- EOD WR: **70.6%** (highest in the month), EOD P&L: +2.90% — small positive (few signals)
- Hold curve: AM weak (-2.11% at +15m), builds through midday, peaks at +5h (+3.49%)
- Context: NK Guam threats (Aug 9-10). VIX spiked briefly to ~16. Market was choppy but tech held up
- **Rule implication**: Low signal count (< 1.0/day) + positive EOD WR = low-activity caution; not a bear regime but not a trend month either. Hold to +4h/+5h rather than EOD.

### September 2017 — Second September Exception
- EOD WR: 44.1%, EOD P&L: +2.68% — positive despite sub-50% WR (avg win >> avg loss)
- Hold curve is choppy: positive at +15m, dips to -2.28% at +2h, spikes at +3h (+5.87%), then fades to EOD
- Context: September 2017 S&P actually rose ~2%. The "September effect" did NOT materialize. FAANG stocks continued to grind higher.
- This is the **second September exception** in the dataset (after Sep 2019 which was also an EV exception)
- Updated September rule: **7/9 years are bear/fade; 2 confirmed exceptions when macro is benign and FAANG in sustained uptrend**
- The positive P&L despite low WR means avg win was ~1.5x avg loss — same Low-WR Positive EV pattern

### October 2017 — Consistent Bull Across All Windows
- All 12 windows from +15m to EOD are positive: +9.49% (+15m) → +16.23% (+4h) → +16.05% (EOD)
- +15m WR: 62.5% — starts strong and stays strong; no AM pop-fade trap
- This is the cleanest "all-day bull" October in the dataset — no fade, no reversal
- **October bull confirmed: 8/9 years positive** (only 2022 was negative in the bear market)
- Context: Earnings season (FAANG Q3 2017 earnings all strong). Tax reform optimism. Steady grinding uptrend.

### November 2017 — Choppy Month, Near-Flat
- EOD WR: 44.0%, EOD P&L: +0.84% — technically positive but barely
- Best window: +1h (64.0% WR, +2.27%) — hold to +1h and exit
- Curve: rises to +1h, dips mid-day (-2.76% at +3h), recovers EOD (+0.84%)
- Context: Senate tax reform debates; market churn while investors waited for clarity
- This is a "wait and see" month where the OR signal fires but no clean directional trend emerges

### December 2017 — Near-Flat Bear (Third December Exception)
- EOD WR: 50.0%, EOD P&L: **-0.76%** — technically negative but essentially flat
- Best windows: +1h30m (+7.12%), +2h (+6.57%) — mid-day hold optimal; EOD fades
- Context: Trump tax cut passed Dec 22. Market had priced in the rally; late-Dec saw profit taking
- This is **NOT a catastrophic December** — it's near-flat, similar to Dec 2019 (+0.69%, macro-catalyst exception)
- **December bear rule updated: 6/9 years clearly negative; Dec 2017 is mild (-0.76%); 2019/2020 are catalyst bulls**
- The mid-day exit signal (+1h30m/+2h) was the right call; holding to EOD gave back all gains

## 2017 Summary for Cross-Year Comparison

- **Year structure:** True two-halves year — Jan-Feb flat/bull, Mar-May bear window, Jun-Jul strong bull, Aug-Sep noise, Oct-Dec tailing off
- **Strongest month:** July 2017 (+21.13% EOD) — rising-curve bull with +15m as shakeout; holding EOD was by far the best choice
- **Second September exception confirmed:** Sep 2017 was positive (+2.68%) despite typical fade pattern — FAANG in uptrend overrides the seasonal effect; now 7/9 bear vs 2/9 exception
- **April joins the persistent-bear list:** 5/9 Aprils are now negative; 2017 is the first where even +15m was losing
- **December 2017 near-flat:** -0.76% is not catastrophic; the mid-day peak (+7.12% at +1h30m) was the exit signal
- **Low-vol environment effect:** Even the "bear" months (Apr, May) were milder than 2018/2022 equivalents; July bull was amplified by persistently low VIX
- **2017 gross EOD (all months long):** +48.61% (sum of all monthly EOD P&Ls — excellent raw year)
- **Strategy would apply:** Jan EOD, Feb +15m, Mar +15m/+30m, Apr skip, May skip, Jun-Jul EOD, Aug +5h, Sep +15m (EV positive), Oct EOD, Nov +1h, Dec +1h30m → estimated strategy yield ~+65% (vs raw +49% pure long)
