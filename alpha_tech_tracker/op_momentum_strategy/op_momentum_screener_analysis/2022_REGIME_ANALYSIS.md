## Key Regime Patterns

### January 2022 — Worst Month in the Full Dataset (2020–2026)
- Signal count: 74 (3.7/day) — one of the highest counts across all years
- EOD WR: 33.8%, Total P&L: **-116.3%** — by a wide margin the single worst month in the entire dataset
- Hold curve: relentlessly negative at every window, worst at +7h (-116.3%)
- Avg loss at EOD: -3.39%, avg win: +2.00% — the downside moves are 70% larger than upside
- Context: January 2022 was the opening month of the Fed-driven tech selloff; the OR breakout signal was generating false entries on every violent downday

**Short strategy would have returned +116.3% in a single month.** The key characteristic is: high signal count + very low EOD WR + declining win rate at every hold window = maximum short edge.

### April 2022 — AM Fake-Out / All-Day Fade
- +15m WR: 59.0% — morning looked bullish
- EOD WR: 33.3% — collapsed all day
- The pattern mirrors Apr 2024 (which was -14.1%) but far more extreme (-47.5%)
- Every hold window from +30m onward is progressively worse — this is the "morning fakeout + relentless fade" pattern
- **Strategy:** The +15m signal was worth taking (+6.1% total), but holding ANY longer was deeply destructive

### July 2022 — Bear-Market Rally (Strongest Summer Bull)
- EOD WR: 70.0% — tied with Apr 2025 for highest across all years
- Hold curve monotonically rises: 50.0% (+15m) → 65.0% (+1h) → 70.0% (EOD)

- Avg win: +2.47%, avg loss: -2.99% — high volatility, but wins dominate
- Pre-session top-2: 60.0% EOD, +27.6% — perfectly aligned with signal analysis
- Context: The Federal Reserve pivot-hope rally (July 2022) was sharp and clean

### November 2022 — Post-CPI Pivot Bull
- Only 28 signals (1.3/day) — selective, high quality
- EOD WR: 64.3%, total P&L: +39.8%

### September 2022 — Sharpest AM-Pop-Fade in the Dataset

- Context: September 2022 was marked by CPI-day volatility where markets opened strongly (trading on Fed pivot hopes) and then reversed as reality set in

### December 2022 — Deepest December Bear in the Dataset

- Total P&L: -32.6%

## 2022 Summary for Cross-Year Comparison

- **Most extreme year in the dataset** — produced the single worst month (Jan, -116.3%) AND two of the three deepest bear months (Jan, Apr, Dec)
- **Short strategy would have been most valuable:** Jan short +116.3%, Apr +47.5%, Dec +32.6%, Aug +29.2%
- **Bear-market rallies are sharp and tradeable** — Jul (+33.3%) and Nov (+39.8%) both showed clean bull signals even in a down-year context
- **Key lesson:** In a confirmed bear market year, the OR breakout signal fails badly on long side but the short-side flip works powerfully. The high signal count in bear months (Jan: 74) amplifies the loss/gain ratio.
- **Seasonal pattern shifts in a bear year:** October was a midday bull (not a typical AM bull), November was the strongest month (post-CPI), and June was the only modest bull in H1
