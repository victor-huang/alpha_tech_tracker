## New Patterns Found in 2025

### 🆕 Pattern: Midday Bear / U-Curve (Mar 2025)
Not seen in 2026 data. The hold-curve forms a U-shape:
- +15m: 44% (slightly weak, but OK)
- **+45m: 28% (deepest bear — worst window of the entire year across all months)**
- +5h: 62% (strong recovery!)
- EOD: 58% (partial recovery)
- Total P&L despite 58% EOD WR: **-7.8%** — avg loss at EOD is -2.72%, avg win only +1.70%

**Why the EOD win rate is misleading:** The winners are small recoveries; the losers are large gap-and-holds. High win rate does not equal profitable P&L when the loss size significantly exceeds the win size.

**Daily data shows extreme inconsistency:** Mar 4 shows 0/9 at +15m but 8/9 at EOD — the opening range breakouts all reversed immediately, then recovered by close. Mar 6 shows 3/3 at +15m, then 0/3 EOD — the opposite. No reliable intraday signal.

**Pre-session top-2 was the better bear indicator:** 42.9% EOD (-19.4% total) — the historical win-rate rankings correctly flagged the poor environment while signal analysis EOD was misleadingly high at 58%.

**Best strategy:** Use +15m scalp only (get in, take the 44% shot quickly), OR wait until +5h+ to enter long. There is no clean short window either since AM is neutral and EOD partially recovers.

### 🆕 Pattern: AM-Pop Fade (Sep 2025)
The open range breakout fires with full momentum, but it is the EXIT not the entry:
- **+15m: 61.4%** (best short-term reading of the year alongside Oct/Nov)
- +30m: 43.2% (drops 18 points in one interval — the sharpest single-window collapse all year)
- +1h–+3h: 43–32% (continues declining)
- EOD: 45.5%

The +15m edge is real — the OR breakout genuinely holds for the first 15 minutes. But by +30m the market has reversed and the breakout is a fakeout at every longer window.

**Pre-session top-2 shows a split:** AM is weak (40.5% at +15m) but the historical best picks recovered to 54.8% EOD. This means the daily OR breakout is failing, but the underlying tickers that historically build all day are still building — just not from the OR close.

**Best strategy:** Enter at OR-close, exit at +15m (take the 61.4% short-term edge), then exit completely. Do not hold. Alternatively, skip entry entirely and wait for +5h+ to re-enter from a lower basis.

### 🆕 Pattern: High Win Rate / Negative Expected Value (Nov 2025)
Win rate statistics are flattering but P&L tells a different story:
- +15m: 65.2% WR — second-best reading across all 2025 months
- EOD: 54.3% WR — sounds neutral to slightly positive
- **EOD Total P&L: -7.9%** on 46 signals

Why: Avg loss at EOD = -2.82%, avg win = +2.05%. The market is producing large gap-and-reversal moves that wipe out multiple small wins. A 54.3% win rate requires only avg_win / avg_loss > (1-0.543)/0.543 = 0.84 to break even, but here avg_win/avg_loss = 0.73 — it is not enough.

**The pre-session top-2 shows the same trap:** 55.3% EOD WR, but -13.2% total.

**Key lesson: always check expected value (Avg Gain column), not just win rate.** The 2026 framework rule "EOD ≥ 60% → LONG" should include a secondary filter: expected value (avg gain per trade) must also be positive.

## Revised Decision Framework (2025 + 2026 Combined)

```
Each Monday morning — check BOTH the 5-day trailing EOD win rate AND expected value (avg gain):

EOD win rate ≥ 60% AND avg gain > 0 (expected value positive)
  → LONG regime
  → Buy OR breakouts, hold +1h to EOD
  → If hold-curve is rising (Apr 2025 style), hold +4h–+5h for max P&L
  → WARNING: if avg loss >> avg win (like Nov 2025), step back even if WR looks OK

EOD win rate ≥ 55% BUT avg gain ≤ 0 (negative expected value)
  → HIGH WIN RATE TRAP (Nov 2025, May 2025)
  → Reduce size sharply, exit at +15m only
  → Do not hold — the tail losses are larger than wins

EOD win rate 45–55%, avg gain positive
  → NEUTRAL / DRIFT (Jan 2025, Jun 2025)
  → Small size, quick +15m–+30m exits
  → Pre-session top-2 may still work if EV is positive

+15m win rate ≥ 60% but drops sharply to < 45% by +30m
  → AM-POP FADE (Sep 2025)
  → Take the +15m trade only — there IS a short-term edge
  → Exit at +15m, do not hold
  → Pre-session top-2 skip or +5h+ re-entry only

Hold-curve forms a U-shape (neutral AM, deep bear midday, partial recovery EOD)
  → MIDDAY BEAR (Mar 2025)
  → Two options: scalp at +15m only, OR wait until +5h+ to enter
  → Pre-session top-2 EOD WR < 45% is the best early warning here
  → Do not short EOD — the recovery by close limits short-side gains

EOD win rate < 40%, +15m also weak (< 45%), no recovery pattern
  → PERSISTENT BEAR (Dec 2025, Mar 2026)
  → Short OR breakout at bar-3 close, hold to EOD
  → Best window: EOD or +5h

EOD win rate < 40%, +15m around 33% (morning collapse)
  → ACUTE MORNING BEAR (Feb 2026)
  → Short at OR-close, cover by +15m–+1h (dip buyers rescue by +3h)
  → Do NOT hold to EOD — the afternoon recovers

TRANSITION signals (any of these):
  → Single day with ≥70% EOD win rate after 5+ days of <40% → regime flip
  → Pre-session top-2 EOD win rate crosses 60% from below 50%
  → U-curve month followed by next-month +4h/+5h win rate > 65% (Mar→Apr 2025)
  → Extreme event (tariff reversal, policy shock): amplified but same directional rules apply
```

## April 2025: Liberation Day Anatomy

The Apr 9, 2025 tariff pause announcement produced the single most profitable cluster of signals in the entire 2-year dataset:
- Apr 9 individual signal returns: +17.24%, +13.62%, +14.91%, +21.01% (4 signals, all wins)
- Apr 11 follow-through: +2.36%, +0.71%, +1.70%, +1.72%, +4.25%, +2.41% (6/6 wins)
- The signal analysis (momentum confirmation) captured this better than pre-session top-2 (+157% vs +22%)

**Rule:** During high-volatility regime-change events, the OR breakout signal becomes MORE reliable, not less — because the opening range itself becomes the clearest expression of institutional conviction. The signal count drops (only high-confidence setups fire) while magnitude and quality both spike.
