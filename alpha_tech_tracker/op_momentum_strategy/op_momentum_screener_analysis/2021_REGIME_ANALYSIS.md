## Key Patterns

### March 2021 — Growth Rotation Bear (Second Worst Month in Dataset)
- EOD WR: 41.2%, total P&L: **-67.0%** — second only to Jan 2022 (-116.3%)
- Context: 10-year Treasury yields surged, triggering a rotation from growth/tech to value. This screener's growth-tech tickers were hit hard.
- +15m was nearly neutral (49.0%), then worsened progressively: +1h 37.3%, +3h 47.1%, EOD 41.2%
- Avg loss at EOD: **-3.49%** (highest avg loss in 2021) — the downside moves were violent
- Pre-session top-2 showed partial immunity (56.5% EOD, -1.5%) — the historical best picks avoided most of the carnage

- Pre-session was significantly less affected: 50.0% EOD (-4.7%) vs signal analysis (-34.7%)
