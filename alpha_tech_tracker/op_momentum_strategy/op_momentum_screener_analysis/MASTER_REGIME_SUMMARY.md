## Seasonal Patterns — Multi-Year Averages

### January

- Bull in **9/11 years** (2016, 2017, 2018, 2019, 2020, 2021, 2023, 2024, 2025 all positive; only 2022 and 2026 negative)
- 2016 and 2017 both confirmed strong Januaries; even the 2022 bear was visible by day 3 (33.8% WR)
- When January is bear, it is macro-driven (2022: Fed pivot panic, 2026: rotation) and detectable early
- **Default: LONG, but confirm by day 3; flip SHORT immediately if day-3 EOD WR < 45%**

### February

- Mixed and event-driven: 2016 extreme bear (oil crash AM-pop-fade), 2017 flat, 2021/2024 bull, 2020/2022/2026 bear
- Feb 2016 holds the all-time record for AM-pop-fade severity: +17.1% at +15m → -16.0% at EOD (33pp swing)
- No reliable seasonal direction in any of 11 years
- **Default: NEUTRAL, follow prior month's regime; watch for AM-pop-fade if macro is volatile**

### March

- The most dangerous month: 7/11 years negative or flat (2017, 2018, 2019, 2021, 2022, 2025, 2026)
- **Two known bull exceptions with named catalysts:** Mar 2016 (Fed pivot to "patient") and Mar 2020 (COVID volatility)
- Mar 2024 was a mild bull (+7.7%); Mar 2023 near-flat (-4.2%)
- **Default: CAUTION, NO POSITION until day-3 confirmation. Pre-session top-2 < 45% EOD WR = bear signal.**

### April

- Highly mixed across 11 years: 2016 bull (+8.2%), 2017 bear (-10%), 2018 EV-positive (+4.2%), 2019 bear, 2020 flat, 2021 mild, 2022 catastrophic (-47.5%), 2023 flat, 2024 bear (-14.1%), 2025 extreme bull (+157%), 2026 bull (+40.6%)
- NOT a reliable seasonal in either direction
- 2025 and 2026 were event-driven (Liberation Day tariff cycle)
- Low-WR Positive EV confirmed in multiple April years (2016, 2018): check avg win/loss ratio when WR < 50%
- **Default: NO ASSUMPTION — confirm from first 2-3 days**

### May

- Generally positive: 7/9 years positive (2016 +15.7%, 2018 slightly neg, 2021 +11%, 2022 slightly neg, 2025 neg EV)
- 2016's strong May (+15.7%) was AMD-amplified (ex-AMD: +5.3%); actual broad-basket strength was moderate
- **Default: MILD BULL, watch expected value not just win rate**

### June

- Range: -3.2% to +25.7% (reported), but 2016's +25.7% is AMD-driven (ex-AMD: -1.1%)
- Adjusted range: -3.2% to +19.8%, never a deep bear or extreme broad bull
- Hold curve often builds through the day (rising-curve type in bull years)
- **Default: NEUTRAL, let the hold curve confirm direction by day 3**

### July

- Consistently strong when a broader bull regime is in place (2016, 2017, 2018 all +16–21%)
- High variance in later years: Jul 2022 (+33.3% — bear-market rally), Jul 2024 (-8.8%)
- 2016 and 2017 Julys both showed rising hold curves — holding EOD was optimal
- Note: 2016 July is AMD-amplified (+5% ex-AMD; still positive but not record-level)
- **Default: FOLLOW PRIOR MONTH'S REGIME; if Jun was rising-curve bull, hold July EOD**

### August

- Very split: strong bull (2017 +2.9%, 2023 +26.2%, 2025 +21.4%), deep bear (2022 -29.2%, 2024 -28.8%)
- Low signal count (0.7–1.1/day) is typical for August; each signal carries higher-than-average weight
- 2016 and 2017 both slightly positive with low signal counts — consistent with "low activity, slight bull" in calm years
- **Default: CHECK AM-POP-FADE — if +15m strong but drops by +30m, exit at +15m only. If prior July was rising-curve bull, default to EOD hold and monitor.**

### September ⚠️ Systematic Caution (with EV exception)

**7/10 years show EOD bear or fade — the most consistent monthly pattern in the dataset:**

- 2016: -3.3% (bear; +15m +3.3% then fades from +1h)
- 2017: **Exception** — +2.7% EOD, low WR but avg win > avg loss (Low-WR Positive EV)
- 2018: -14.2% (persistent bear, strongest confirmation)
- 2019: **Exception** — +8.3% despite low WR; rising late-day EV
- 2020: extreme AM-pop-fade (66%→34% at +1h)
- 2021: -4.8%, 2022: -7.8%, 2023: -6.8%, 2025: -5.3%
- 2024: neutral (third exception)
- **Bear years: 2016, 2018, 2020, 2021, 2022, 2023, 2025 (7/10). Exceptions: 2017, 2019, 2024 (3/10)**
- **Default: TAKE +15m ONLY (even +15m was positive in 6 of 7 bear Septembers). Exception: if avg win > 1.5× avg loss AND hold curve rising by day 3.**

### October ✅ Reliable Bull

**9/11 years positive (exceptions: 2016 and 2021):**

- 2016: -3.0% (pre-Trump election anxiety — political override)
- 2017: +16.1%, 2018: +8.3% (held despite correction), 2019: +13.7%, 2020: +27.1%
- 2021: -1.5% (Omicron/taper concern)
- 2022: +9.8%, 2023: +10.6%, 2024: +32.2%, 2025: +22.3%
- **Both exceptions have identifiable macro overrides:** 2016 = election uncertainty, 2021 = Omicron. Without a named political/macro event, October is reliably long.
- **Default: LONG from day 1. In correction/political-uncertainty context: take +15m, verify +30m WR > 55% before extending.**

### November

- High variance: Nov 2024 (+42.9%) vs Nov 2021 (-34.7%) vs Nov 2016 (-5.2% Trump shock)
- 2016 was negative because election-day tech rotation hurt the tech-heavy screener even as the broader market rose
- 2017 near-flat (+0.8%) — choppy pre-tax-cut environment
- Bull Novembers (2022, 2023, 2024) have large avg wins; bear Novembers (2016, 2021, 2025) have negative EV
- **Default: VERIFY EV after week 1; if avg gain < 0 per signal, treat as bear regardless of WR**

### December ⚠️ Persistent Bear (with macro catalyst exceptions)

**8/10 years negative (including near-flat 2016 and 2017). Two clear bull exceptions both macro-catalyst driven:**

- 2016: -0.3% (near-flat; post-election rally priced in; peaks at +1h30m)
- 2017: -0.8% (near-flat; tax cut priced in; peaks at +1h30m)
- 2018: -39.8% (worst Dec since 1931; Fed autopilot crash)
- 2019: +9.1% (Phase 1 trade deal Dec 13) ← catalyst override
- 2020: +20.3% (vaccine approval) ← catalyst override
- 2021: -5.4%, 2022: -32.6%, 2023: -2.4%, 2024: -16.3%, 2025: -25.7%
- **Pattern: catastrophic bear in extreme macro bears (2018, 2022, 2025); near-flat when prior month's rally was already priced in (2016, 2017); catalyst-driven bull only with a specific named event (2019, 2020)**
- **Default: REDUCE SIZE. SHORT or +15m exit only. Mid-day exit (+1h30m) is better than EOD in near-flat years.**

### Step 1 — Seasonal Prior

```
January:   Mild LONG prior
March:     CAUTION prior — verify by day 3
September: +15m ONLY prior — exit before +30m
October:   LONG prior
December:  BEAR prior — reduce size
All others: NEUTRAL prior
```

### Step 2 — Five-Day Rolling Check (override seasonal prior if needed)

```
EOD win rate ≥ 60% AND avg gain (EV) > 0
  → LONG regime: hold +1h to EOD; if hold curve is rising, maximize hold to +5h
  
EOD win rate ≥ 55% BUT avg gain ≤ 0 (negative EV despite high WR)
  → HIGH-WR TRAP: exit at +15m only, do NOT hold
  
EOD win rate 45–55%, avg gain positive
  → NEUTRAL: take +15m to +30m profits, no overnight

+15m win rate ≥ 60% but drops ≥15pp by +30m
  → AM-POP-FADE: exit at +15m, this is the only tradeable window

Hold curve U-shape: neutral AM → deep bear midday → partial EOD recovery
  → MIDDAY BEAR: scalp at +15m only OR wait for +5h re-entry; pre-session top-2 EOD < 45% confirms

EOD win rate < 40% AND hold curve declining from short to long
  → PERSISTENT BEAR: short the OR breakout at bar-3 close, hold to EOD

EOD win rate < 40% AND +15m also weak (< 45%), immediate reversal
  → ACUTE AM BEAR: short at OR-close, cover at +15m–+1h (dip buyers rescue by afternoon)
```

### Step 3 — Transition Signals

```
→ Single day with ≥70% EOD win rate after 5+ consecutive days of <40%
→ Pre-session top-2 EOD win rate crosses 60%+ from below 50%
→ Hold curve shape flips from declining to rising over 2–3 consecutive days
```

### Step 4 — Pre-Session Top-2 Independence Rule

The pre-session top-2 (9:25 unconditional entry, 20d EOD WR ranking) can diverge materially from signal analysis:

- **Positive divergence** (pre-session wins when signal fails): seen in Aug 2024, Mar 2025, Mar 2026, Sep 2021 — use pre-session as an independent LONG when signal analysis is in AM-pop-fade or midday-bear mode
- **Negative divergence** (signal wins when pre-session fails): seen in Jan 2021, Aug 2023 — pre-session can lag in sudden bull breakouts
- **When both agree:** strongest confirmation in either direction

#### Bear → Bull (detectable within 1 day — high confidence)

#### Bull → Bear (2–5 day lag — you absorb some losses first)

Three speed categories based on how much warning the data provides:

**Instantaneous (0–1 day):** Feb 2018 (Volmageddon), Dec 2018 week 2, early Jan 2022. The +15m WR on day 1 was already 30–42%. The data confirms within 1 day but the first day's loss is unavoidable. These are macro shocks with no OR-signal-based leading indicator.

**Fast (2–3 day):** Mar 2021, Nov 2021. Negative EV at +15m and a declining hold curve appeared by day 2–3. Catching the signal by day 3 still captures most of the short-side opportunity in months that ran -34% to -67%.

**Gradual (3–5 day):** Sep 2018, Sep 2020, Sep 2022, Sep 2025. The classic fade: morning signals look acceptable, but +30m-to-+1h erosion builds progressively. The bear is unambiguous by day 4–5.

**Detection signals in order of leading time:**

1. **Signal count spike + WR collapse** — high count (>2/day) with EOD WR < 40% within 2–3 days; the most actionable early signal
2. **Hold curve shape by day 3** — if +3h and +5h P&L are both negative while +15m is near zero, the month is bear
3. **Avg loss approaching avg win** — when avg loss / avg win > 0.80, EV turns negative before EOD WR confirms it
4. **Pre-session top-2 using stale lookback** — in fast regime changes the pre-session fires on prior-month winners that are now losers; do NOT trust pre-session picks in the first week after a macro regime change

### Where the Strategy Works vs Where It Breaks

**Works reliably:**

- Scheduled bear months (Sep, Dec): 75% accuracy with seasonal prior alone, before any signal check
- Bear-to-bull confirmation: one 70%+ day after a confirmed bear has zero false positives across 9 years
- Hold window selection: rising-curve rule has never mislead; AM-pop-fade has been consistent every year since 2018
- October: the single most reliable seasonal in the dataset — correct 7/8 years including in the 2018 correction

**Does not work — honest limitations:**

- **Instantaneous macro shocks** (Feb 2018 Volmageddon, Dec 2018 week 2, Mar 2021 yield spike): No leading indicator from OR data. The data confirms in 1–2 days but the first day's loss is unavoidable. Accept this as the cost of operating in a signal-based system.
- **November** is genuinely unpredictable from any seasonal rule — the spread (+42.9% in 2024 vs -34.7% in 2021) is the widest of any month and depends entirely on macro context. Use the EV check, not a seasonal prior.
- **August** is the least reliable month. The bull/bear split (2021 +15%, 2023 +26%, 2025 +21% vs 2022 -29%, 2024 -29%) has no identifiable prior-month pattern. Trade smaller or wait for the first-week shape.
- **Timing within-day regime changes from rolling data**: The signal lag (3+ days needed to confirm) is too long to time intraday entry/exit changes. Don't try to flip hold windows mid-day based on a pattern that needs days to confirm.
