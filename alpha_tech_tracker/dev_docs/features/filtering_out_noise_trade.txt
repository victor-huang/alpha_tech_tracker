=========Big gap up day no trade at the begining

⏺ Here's why all signals were NEUTRAL on April 8 at 9:47:            

  BULLISH impossible — On a crash day, all tickers opened in the lower half of their OR (close < midpoint) and below MA200. Both BULLISH conditions failed immediately.                             
   
  BEARISH blocked by MA20 — This is the real issue. The BEARISH condition requires:                                                                                                                 
  close <= bottom_30  # close ≤ or_low + 20% × or_range  ✓ (prices in bottom of OR)
  close < ma20_d      # ✗ — this is what failed                                                                                                                                                     
                                                                                                                                                                                                    
  MA20 covers only the last 20 five-minute bars (~100 minutes of real-time data). At 9:40 ET on April 8, that MA20 was averaging 3 new opening bars + 17 bars from April 7 afternoon (~2:15–3:55    
  PM). After multiple days of tariff-driven selling, those April 7 afternoon bars were already at depressed levels — meaning MA20 had already declined to approximately the April 8 opening prices. 
  So close < MA20 was false: the stock was trading at or above its recent short-term average.                                                                                                       
                                                                                                                                                                                                    
  This is the strategy's momentum filter working as designed. It only fires BEARISH when the stock is breaking down below its recent 100-min average — confirming new bearish momentum. On a        
  prolonged multi-day selloff, MA20 chases price down, and by the time price reaches bottom-of-OR on day N, MA20 has already caught up. There's no "new breakdown" to confirm.
                                                                                                                                                                                                    
  The regime filter is not the cause — it only suppresses BULLISH signals on bearish regime dates, not BEARISH ones.                                                                                
                                               
  Potential fix if you want to capture these days: Lower or remove the MA20 requirement for BEARISH signals, or use a longer MA (MA50/MA200) as the bearish momentum gate, which would lag further  
  behind and still be above the crash-day opening prices.
