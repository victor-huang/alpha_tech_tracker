here is the rule for the first 15 - 30 minutes price volume momentum indicator(let's call it op_momentum_guide):
the first 15 or 20 minutes set the price range, at the end of the period, if stock price is above the mid-point of the range, it is bullish indicator, if the price close at the bottom 10% of the range, it is bearish indicator.
we will combine this with the moving average lines, if price is above the 20-period moving average, it is bullish indicator, if price is below the 20-period moving average, it is bearish indicator. and the price needs to be above 200-period moving average for bullish signal to be valid. for bearish signals, the price needs to be below 200-period moving average.
